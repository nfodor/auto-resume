- [founder,cto,product] Invented voice-to-internet messaging — the world's first mobile internet technology
- [product,founder] First deployed at ZAZ (1995), Brazil's — and the world's — largest ISP, for all their users
- [cto,product] Acquired and launched as Voxmail by SFR/Vodafone, then scaled globally as Vodafone Live! serving millions
- [cto,architect,dev] Bridged early internet protocols (SMTP/POP3) with telephony voice interfaces for mass-market adoption
- [founder,cto] Pioneered convergent communications years before unified messaging became an industry
