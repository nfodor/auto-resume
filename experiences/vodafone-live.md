- [founder,cto,product] Invented "Email by Voice" — voice-to-internet messaging, the world's first mobile internet technology
- [product,founder] First deployed at ZAZ (1995), Brazil's — and the world's — largest ISP, for all their users
- [cto,architect] Launched as Voxmail (1997), the first mobile internet service by a mobile operator (SFR/Vodafone)
- [architect,cto,product] Scaled globally as Vodafone Live!, the first global mobile internet, for the world's largest mobile operator
- [product,cto] Defined the mobile internet user experience before the smartphone era, integrating WAP, MMS, and content services into a unified portal
- [sre,architect,cto] Scaled the service to serve millions of users, becoming the de-facto mobile internet standard across Europe and the globe
- [cto,founder,ceo,cofounder] Delivered a category-defining product for one of the world's largest mobile operators
